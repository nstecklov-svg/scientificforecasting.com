How SFC forecasts

Beyond the headlines.

A comprehensive historical database we call "the brain" lets the model research far deeper than standard prediction bots. Next, the model will be able to ask itself questions and find its own answers in the brain, mimicking how humans forecast while weighing thousands more pieces of information than a human could.

The news.

Using AskNews, our model gathers roughly 20 relevant articles from trusted news sources, then reads, analyzes and weighs them before reaching a conclusion.

Our own model.

The model is trained by rewarding accuracy, measured with Brier scores, the industry standard for scoring predictions. It has already been trained and scored on over a thousand real questions.

At a glance

14B parameters in our model.
~10,000 questions to train on.
~1,000 H100 GPU hours of training.
~6 weeks of training.
~40,000 practice forecasts written and scored.
~200,000 news articles read during training.
Covers politics, economics, and world events.

Figures are estimates for our next training run.
