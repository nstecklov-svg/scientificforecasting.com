How SFC forecasts

Beyond the headlines.

Forming a comprehensive historical database called "the brain" allows the model to research far deeper than standard prediction bots. Letting the model ask itself questions and find it's own answers allows it to mimic human forecasting while being able to weigh thousands of more pieces of information than a human could.

The news.

Using AskNews, our model grabs roughly 20 relevant and trusted news sources, reading, analyzing and weighing the information it gathers before forming a conclusion.

Our own model.

The model is rewarded for being accurate, using Brier scores, the industry standard for predictions. Thousands of questions have been tested and scored already.

At a glance

14B parameters in our model.
~10,000 questions to train on.
~1,000 H100 GPU hours of training.
~6 weeks of training.
~40,000 practice forecasts written and scored.
~200,000 news articles read during training.
Covers politics, economics, and world events.

Figures are estimates for our next training run.
